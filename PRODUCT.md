# GlassScale

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
A Chinese personal iPhone app for quickly logging weight and water, reviewing change over time and backfilling missed days. Offline local data and optional Apple Health integration.

## Approved UI direction
Preserve the existing ocean blue/mint identity and iOS 26 native Liquid Glass navigation. Weight is the dashboard's primary reading; goal progress is secondary. Show water immediately after the weight section. Expose weight/water and calendar/list controls on Records.

## Principles
Readable quiet content surfaces, familiar native actions, visible timestamps, compact summaries and accessible text resizing. Leave floating tab geometry, refraction and system gestures to native TabView. User data, reminders, existing controls and backfill rules remain intact.

## Scope
UI trial on ui/floating-glass-preview; no merge to main without user direction. Reference is the two-screen concept approved in this chat, with the corrected detached floating native bottom bar. No new body-fat, account, report or widget features.
